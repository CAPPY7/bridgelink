# User guide

BridgeLink 1.1.1 links one or more Android phones to a Mac on the menu bar. The browser page is a simulation. It is not connected to your devices.

## First open

On the Mac, a setup window walks through notifications, the local network, launch at login, and pairing. You can open it again from Settings, Show setup again.

On the phone, the first open is one page per permission. Each page has Allow and Skip. Nothing is asked until you tap Allow. At the end, Settings, Permissions, shows On, Off, or Not needed on this phone. Show setup again is in Settings.

On the Mac, the setup window also explains Finder. Right-click a file, then Quick Actions or Services, then Send to phone. The same steps are used on macOS 13, 14, and 15. BridgeLink must be in Applications. Open Keyboard Shortcuts from the setup window or from Settings if the item is missing. The app refreshes Services when it opens, so you do not need to log out.

## Day to day

- Pair on the same Wi-Fi. Scan the Mac QR. That is the first button on the phone. The code lasts 2 minutes.
- Send a note, a link, or files either way. Drop files on the Mac window or the menu-bar icon.
- Clipboard can stay in sync. Passwords and card numbers are skipped. Both apps keep the last 100 copies.
- Notifications can mirror, with reply when the phone app allows it. Texts can be read and sent from the Mac after you allow SMS. Files stay with files. There is no combined list.
- The phone can be a trackpad, keyboard, and slide remote. The Mac needs Accessibility.
- Mirror shows the phone screen in a Mac window. Closing it stops the picture. Clicks in that window need the phone accessibility service.
- Webcam shows the phone camera inside BridgeLink. It does not appear in Zoom unless you have a paid Apple Developer ID.
- If you turn it on, a weak Bluetooth signal locks the Mac. The phone never unlocks it. It is off after a fresh install.
- A ringing call can show on the Mac, with Decline, after you turn call alerts on.
- History on the phone searches files, links, and notes. Secrets are not stored.
- Photos on the Mac shows recent phone pictures, 30 at a time, after you turn phone photos on.
- The menu bar shows phone battery, signal bars, and the latest notification title. It is a panel, not a home-screen widget.
- Pause phone notifications is separate from quiet Mac banners while the phone is in Do Not Disturb.
- Text from photo reads words on the phone and can send them to the Mac clipboard. It does not use the network.
- A scan opens on the Mac only when Open scans automatically is on and the phone marked that file as a scan. The switch stays off until you turn it on.
- The apps link only on the same Wi-Fi. There is no remote path.

## Updates and reset

Settings, Check for update, reads the release list. If a newer version exists, it downloads the disk image, checks the hash, and opens it. You install the APK over the old one, or replace the Mac app in Applications.

Reset BridgeLink forgets paired devices and saved history. On the Mac it also drops the Mac key. Quit, open BridgeLink again, then pair. Dragging the Mac app to the Trash is enough to uninstall. Reset does not remove the app.

## What still needs you

- macOS 15 shows Open Anyway once. The install page has the steps.
- Android 13 and newer may need Allow restricted settings before notification access, texts, or screen control.
- Leave-lock needs Bluetooth on the phone.
- Files over 32 MB in the shared folder are skipped. Other sends can be larger, up to 4 GB.
